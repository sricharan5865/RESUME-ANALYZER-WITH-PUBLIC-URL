--- Slide 4 ---
[TextBox 1]: THE KEYWORD GAP
[TextBox 2]: The Keyword Trap: Legacy ATS Limitations
[TextBox 4]: LEGACY KEYWORD FILTERS
Rigid and Non-Intuitive
• Syntactic Matching: Rejects candidates who use synonyms instead of exact keywords (e.g. searching 'NodeJS' misses 'Express Developer').• Keyword Stuffing: Unqualified candidates game the system by pasting hidden keywords in their CV text.• No Understanding of Context: Treats a keyword in a hobby section same as one in a principal role.
[TextBox 6]: TALENTFLOW SEMANTIC RAG
Conceptual Intelligence
• Vector Embeddings: Understands core concepts, related technical packages, and equivalent roles natively.• Senses Experience Depth: Prioritizes skills based on time duration and impact descriptions in previous roles.• Intent-Based Queries: Search matches are returned based on what recruiters *mean*, not just the specific words they type.

--- Slide 5 ---
[TextBox 1]: RELIABILITY RISKS
[TextBox 2]: The AI Deficit: LLM Hallucinations & Date Math
[TextBox 4]: THE PROBLEM WITH RAW LLMS
Hallucinations & Date Bugs
• Static Context: LLMs don't know 'today's date' natively. They miscalculate durations for roles ending in 'Present'.• Syntactic Math Failures: AI models fail to compute sum timelines properly, flagging a candidate with 8 years of experience as missing a '5+ years' requirement.• Structural Failures: Raw completions truncate output or fail to return valid JSON parser schemas.
[TextBox 6]: TALENTFLOW SAFEGUARDS
Structured Engineering Rules
• Dynamic Date Injection: Injecting context date on every API call allows the parser to compute relative dates reliably.• Programmatic Safety Filters: Post-processing code intercepts and validates LLM findings against mathematical candidate histories.• Hardened Token Configurations: Limits outputs and applies robust bracket repair algorithms to handle parsing mistakes.

[TextBox 1]: OUR PRODUCT
[TextBox 2]: The TalentFlow Solution: Resilient Recruitment
[TextBox 4]: THE RECRUITMENT OPERATING SYSTEM
A Reliable, Safe, and Automated Platform
• Enterprise Integration: Collects resumes, normalizes data, and publishes structured files to MongoDB.• High-Performance UI: Glassmorphism HUD layout that handles large tables, date filtering, and active tabs natively.• Adaptive Intelligence: Switch backend models dynamically based on cost or speed demands (Gemini, Claude, Ollama).• Recruiter Control: Empowering decisions via transparent ratings, complete history log tracking, and security role controls.

[TextBox 6]: JOB DESCRIPTION MATCHING
JD Scoring & Skill Mapping
• Real-Time JD Matching: Pasting a JD ranks candidates by score (0-100), comparing resume content against required credentials.• Skills Gap Analysis: The UI renders color-coded badges highlighting 'Matching Skills' and 'Missing Skills' side-by-side.• Auto-generated JD Questions: Produces custom interview questions focused on resolved gaps.

--- Slide 9 ---
[TextBox 1]: SAFETY INTERCEPTOR
[TextBox 2]: Feature Spotlight: Algorithmic Safety Filters
[TextBox 4]: DETERMINISTIC VERIFICATION
Curing LLM Hallucinations
• Safety Layer Interception: Overrides LLM outputs using local Javascript date-math functions on candidate history arrays.• Dynamic Experience Alignment: Mathematically confirms timeline values (e.g. if timeline equals 8 years, auto-moves '5+ years' requirement to 'Matches').• Recruiter Validation: Ensures zero false negatives, preventing valid candidates from being hidden from recruiter pipelines.
[TextBox 6]: THE MATHEMATICAL GUARANTEE
Reliable Timeline Math
• Injects 'today's date' context dynamically to parse relative date keywords (e.g. 'Present').• Sanitizes candidate profiles before saving them to MongoDB, correcting duration errors.• Decouples unstructured LLM reasoning from strict business logic constraints, maintaining high system trust.
